Computer Systems Manager

E.T. GO HOME!

Computer Systems Managers, or CSMs, sit at the top of the information technology chain, monitoring networks and data
processing systems, managing funds and the hardware those funds go into maintaining, and all the while coaching their
colleagues. Like any other IT job, there will be more than plenty of ιd1φt errors, but that's simply the ups and downs of
the trade.

What Do They Do?

CSMs' tasks can be split into three categories:

  • Hardware Tasks (upgrades, repairs, maintenance)
  • Software Tasks (OS updates, internal errors, failures to boot)
  • Social Tasks (coaching colleagues, coordinating projects, reviewing budgets)
Each one is important in different ways and at different times, but all three are necessary to be performed.
