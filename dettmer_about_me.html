About Me, The Long And... Nope, Just The Long Of It

  • I lived in Ogdensburg for my first five years, before moving into Lisbon, where I've been living ever since. My family had almost no
    technological affiliation until my father went into data science and joined Crawford Technologies in Potsdam. One of my most vivid memories of
    Ogdensburg is happily pretending to work in his closet with a broken laptop, while he did the actual work behind me. Good times.

  • I'm now sixteen, as a junior in LCS. I chose the CIS program because of the numerous different directions I could take it, and because I'm
    already familiar with the structure of programming. After CIS, my favorite class would be, predictably, orchestral band, though I'm always a sucker
    for marching band season when it comes around. I often run the light systems for the school's Drama Club, and sound when it's needed for other events.

  • After school, I'm looking at going to college, likely for something computer-oriented, here at SUNY Canton. There's plenty of careers that are
    interesting to me, but in general, I want to get out there and help make things. The feeling of putting something together and having it just
    work is immensely satisfying. In ten years, I'd like to at least have a stable home, and a good job. I don't want to shoot for
    anything too huge.

My Personal Favorite Things

Specifically, music and games.

  • Games (Top 3):
    1. Tom Clancy's The Division 2
      This is one of those RPG games where everything is customizable, and you can basically make your character good at just about anything, from
      having immensely powerful drones to being a walking block of tungsten. I love it, and keep coming back to it even now, eight years after its release.
    2. Hollow Knight
      I'm an absolute slave to a well-fleshed-out world, and this metroidvania from 2017 simply does exactly that for me. Despite my preferences in music
      being, well, the absolute opposite direction from soft orchestral pieces, the soundtrack is still something special.
    3. Factorio
      I have a love-hate relationship with this game at the moment, between my save files corrupting twice in a row now, and this game's ability to forcibly
      take my spare time from me almost at will. I didn't think I would be a Factorio lover. Then I played Factorio. Now all I can think when a problem
      comes up is, "man, I wish I could automate that."
  • Artists (Top 3):
    1. Sleep Token
      They became big in 2023 with a massive hit, and that was when I found them. Their music branches in so many different directions while still being
      metal at the foundation. If I had to think of one word for what they do best, it would be soundscapes. Even writing this now, I have "Emergence" in
      my headphones.
    2. Disturbed
      A lot of my music taste is fairly dated, but for good reason. Disturbed was one of the biggest influences on rock and metal as a whole during the
      2000's, and even today, I can't help but love it. It's everything that decade was for music.
    3. Avenged Sevenfold
      Another huge player during the 2000's and 2010's, their sound is easily recognizable once you know any of their music. It's becoming steadily apparent
      that my head's in the 2000's while my body's stuck here in 2026. Man, what I wouldn't do for a time machine sometimes.

Favorite Pastimes

  • Drumming
    Drumming has been huge for me. Generally, if I go a day without practicing, then something's clearly wrong. I've always managed to get a feel for it, and I
    don't think it'll be going away any time soon.

  • Games
    This probably should come to no surprise, considering how much I gushed about games in the section farther up the page, but a lot of my time is spent
    playing a good few games. Whether I'm getting frustrated in Kerbal Space Program, or making other people frustrated in Modern Warfare (2019), I've
    put way too many hours into sitting on the couch for my own good.

  • Books
    In an age where everyone's turning away from physical pen and paper, I'm that one guy whose bedroom wall is undisputably conquered by books. Harry
    Potter, Wings of Fire, The Missing trilogy, LOTR, Artemis Fowl,     and probably a few dozen more that I can't remember off the top of my head. It's the
    world I was raised in, and I wouldn't have it any other way.

Interesting Tech Topics

  • NHRL Official Website - the NHRL, or the National Havoc Robot League, hosted in Norwalk, Conneticut, is essentially a robotics nerd's heaven, where
    you build machines of destruction and promptly throw the at each other for the sake of science and fun. Again, the idea of building something, testing it,
    and then pitting it head-to-head against someone else's thing entices me greatly.

  • 3D Printing is something that is huge in my house. Having a Bambu Lab P1S with four filament slots, we always have at least one project running. This
    last week, it was a magnetic GoPro mount for my father's FJ Cruiser. On the topic of Bambu Lab, they host their own 3D filesharing site, Makerworld,
    where individual printing files for your specific printer can be found.
