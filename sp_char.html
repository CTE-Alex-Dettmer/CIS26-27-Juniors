HTML Special Characters

Entity: this is a mechanism for writing special characters or symbols in HTML, such as mathematical symbols, characters in most languages, and many
other symbols. Many HTML entities can be specified by name, by a decimal number, or by a hexadecimal number.

E.T. GO HOME!

Example: a copyright symbol.
© - &copy;
© - &#169;
© - &#x000A9;

Emojis

Emoji characters can be displayed in a webpage with a decimal or hexadecimal entity. Example: the happy face. (Something I lack when undercaffeinated.)
😀 (&#128512;) OR: 😀 (&#x1F600;)

Entities for Reserved Characters

The < and > characters require entities to be safely and properly displayed on the webpage. (&lt; and &gt;) The & character requires its own, too. (&amp;)
Already, we've used it at least ten times throughout this HTML file, and unless you inspect the page, you'll never know.

Non-Breaking Characters

A non-breaking character is an inter-word character that permits treating the words on both sides to be one word.

Non-Breaking Hyphen

A non breaking hyphen, ‑ looks like a regular hyphen but acts like a normal character in the middle of a word. (&#8209;)

Non-Breaking Space

A non-breaking space ( ) looks like a single space but acts like a normal character in the middle of a word. This is my wo  rd. (&nbsp;)

WARNING: Do NOT use non-breaking spaces to create space for the Layout of your page.
